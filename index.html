Business-data assistants · retrieval · access control

AI answers grounded in business data

An internal assistant needs to use the right data, respect access rules, and leave a trace of its actions.

I built full-stack features connecting an AI assistant to account information, warehouse data, documents, and application tools.

  • Controlled actions: permission-filtered tools, validated inputs, execution limits, and cancellation.
  • Useful business context: account views, Salesforce workflows, Snowflake data, and search over related records.
  • Visibility after launch: conversation and tool-call records, token and estimated-cost tracking, user feedback, and admin review.
What I implemented and why it matters

I built a shared AI execution loop, repeated-call protection, conversation records, answer feedback, and administrator screens. My data work included account lists and filters, role-based data masking, and automated email briefings.

These features help a business understand what an assistant did, limit which actions it can take, and improve poor answers using actual feedback.
